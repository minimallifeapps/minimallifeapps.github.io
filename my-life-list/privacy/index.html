MY LIFE LIST_

Privacy Policy · Effective October 19, 2026

My Life List does not collect any data. Nothing you write ever leaves your phone.

What the app stores

The entries you write and two settings (whether the lock is on, and whether you have seen the intro) are saved only on your device, inside the app's private storage. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No sync, cloud backup service, or servers of our own.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising, or third-party SDKs.
  • No network requests of any kind.

Face ID

If you turn on the lock, the app asks iOS to confirm it's you. Face ID, Touch ID, and your passcode are handled entirely by Apple's iOS. The app never sees or stores any biometric data; it only receives a yes or no.

Device backups

Like any app's data, your list may be included in an iCloud or computer backup of your iPhone if you have backups turned on. Those backups are controlled by you and Apple, not by us.

Deleting your data

Delete an entry in the app to remove it. Delete the app to remove everything permanently.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
